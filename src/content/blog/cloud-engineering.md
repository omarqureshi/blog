Cloud Engineering has evolved from simply migrating VMs to the cloud, to adopting cloud-native patterns like microservices, serverless, and event-driven architectures.

## The Shift to Serverless

Managing infrastructure is becoming a thing of the past for many application developers. With services like AWS Lambda, Fargate, and Step Functions, we can build scalable systems without ever touching an operating system.

In future posts, I'll dive deep into specific AWS CDK patterns to build robust cloud infrastructure.
